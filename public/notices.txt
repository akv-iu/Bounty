Map: projected US state geometry retained from the accepted UI prototype,
derived from US Atlas v3 / US Census Bureau cartography.
US Atlas geometry is public domain: https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas
The static SVG replaces a runtime D3/TopoJSON dependency for this fixed map.
Pins are approximate simulated compute pools, not verified cloud sites or grid boundaries.

Historical carbon: U.S. Energy Information Administration Grid Monitor,
hourly consumption emissions estimates, July 2, 2026.
Source and conversion details are recorded in data/README.md in the repository.
Prices, capacity and workloads are synthetic. Calculated results are not realized savings.
